Who we are

Vision & Mission

We work with government departments and private businesses alike — taking the paperwork nobody has time to deal with and handing back tidy, dependable digital records.

Our Vision

Scanning shouldn't stop at pictures of paper. We want every file we touch to come back as something you can search, share and build on — records that work for your organisation instead of taking up shelf space.

Our Mission

  • Return every batch accurate, complete and on schedule.
  • Keep training our people, so quality never depends on luck.
  • Put modern scanners and software to work for every client.

What we do

Our Services

From the first box of files to the final digital handover, we manage every step: preparation, scanning, quality checks, OCR, indexing and delivery straight into your business systems.

Data Processing

Paper archives and loose files become clean, structured data. Our software reads each document, sorts it, pulls out the fields that matter and checks them — then loads the results straight into your database, ERP or CRM.

Data Conversion

Old paper records, legacy media and image files converted into tidy, database-ready formats that your current systems can actually use.

Document Preparation & Logging

Every bundle is sorted, unstapled, counted and logged before it goes near a scanner — and accounted for again when it comes back to you.

High-Resolution Imaging & QC

Fast, sharp scans followed by a manual quality check and image cleanup — so what you see on screen is as legible as the original, often better.

OCR & Metadata Indexing

We run text recognition over every page and tag it with the details you search by — then deliver the files securely and handle the originals however you ask.
